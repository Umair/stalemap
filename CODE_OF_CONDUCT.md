# Code of conduct

stalemap follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

To report a problem with someone's conduct in this project, contact the maintainer privately at umairejaz.ch@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential.
