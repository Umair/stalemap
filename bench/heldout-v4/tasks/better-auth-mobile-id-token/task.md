Our mobile app signs users in to Acme SSO natively and then posts the Acme id_token to better-auth's social sign-in endpoint with provider `acme`; right now that doesn't sign anyone in. Update the Acme provider in `src/acme.ts` so those id_tokens are accepted only when they're signed with one of Acme's keys (published at `<issuer>/.well-known/jwks.json`), were issued by the configured issuer and were issued for our client id.
