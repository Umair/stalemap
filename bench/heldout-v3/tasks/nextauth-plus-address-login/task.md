People end up with two accounts because they sign in with plus-addressing (`ada+shop@example.com` and `ada@example.com`). Change the email provider in `src/auth.ts` so a `+tag` in the local part is dropped from the identifier, which is what we look users up by and send the sign-in link to. Apart from that, identifiers must be normalized and validated exactly as next-auth's built-in normalizer does.
